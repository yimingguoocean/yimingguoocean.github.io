I am an Assistant Professor at Illinois State University studying the physical processes that govern oceans, lakes, and the climate system. My research combines satellite remote sensing, in situ observations, reanalysis products, and high-resolution numerical models to investigate water dynamics, biogeochemistry, and climate variability across scales. I am particularly interested in ocean and lake dynamics, regional and global climate, air–water interactions, carbon cycling, and nature-based climate solutions. I welcome inquiries from prospective graduate students, postdoctoral researchers, and collaborators interested in these topics.
