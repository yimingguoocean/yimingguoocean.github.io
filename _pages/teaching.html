I teach interdisciplinary courses in ocean, climate, weather, and water science that connect physical processes with real-world environmental challenges. My teaching emphasizes scientific literacy, data analysis, critical thinking, and the use of observations, models, and visual tools to understand how Earth’s water, atmosphere, and climate systems work together.

GEO/SOC 103 – Thirsty Society: An Interdisciplinary Examination of Water

This course explores water as a global sustainability issue, including climate regulation, food and agriculture, flooding and drought, public health, and water access.
